为什么“耶稣的名”成为他们信息的中心？ Why did “the name of Jesus” become central to their message?

在使徒行传里，“耶稣的名”不是一句魔法口令。这个“名”代表祂是谁、祂的权柄、祂所成就的事，以及人对祂的信靠。 In Acts, “the name of Jesus” is not a magic formula. The name represents who He is, His authority, what He accomplished, and trust placed in Him.

彼得宣告，这位耶稣就是被钉十字架、又被神从死里复活的那一位（徒 4:10）。换句话说，他们不是为一个抽象宗教品牌受苦，而是为一个他们确信仍然活着的人作见证。 Peter declared that this Jesus was the One crucified and then raised from the dead by God (Acts 4:10). In other words, they were not suffering for an abstract religious brand, but testifying about a person they were convinced was alive.

这就是为什么彼得能说：“我们所看见、所听见的，不能不说。”（徒 4:20）对他们而言，沉默反而意味着否认自己所见证的事实。 That is why Peter could say, “We cannot but speak the things which we have seen and heard” (Acts 4:20). For them, silence would have meant denying what they believed they had witnessed.

Acts 4:12
彼得的中心宣告 Peter’s central claim

“除他以外，别无拯救，因为在天下人间，没有赐下别的名，我们可以靠着得救。”

“Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.”

这与今天的我们有什么关系？ What does this mean for us today?

从故事走向问题 From story to question
01 / 先认识祂01 / Know Him

耶稣不只是宗教符号Jesus is more than a religious symbol

基督信仰首先提出的，不是一套道德规则，而是一个人：耶稣基督。问题是：祂到底是谁？ Christian faith begins not merely with a moral code, but with a person: Jesus Christ. The question is: who is He?

02 / 面对复活的宣告02 / Face the resurrection claim

彼得和约翰把一切押在复活上Peter and John staked everything on resurrection

如果耶稣没有复活，他们的见证就失去中心；如果祂真的复活，那么祂对生命、死亡和救恩的意义就不能轻易被忽略。 If Jesus did not rise, their testimony loses its center. If He truly rose, His significance for life, death, and salvation cannot be easily dismissed.

03 / 救主03 / Savior

基督徒相信：耶稣也是我们的救主Christians believe Jesus is our Savior too

使徒行传 4:12 把救恩指向耶稣。基督徒相信，祂不仅是彼得和约翰所见证的主，也是今天人可以信靠的救主。 Acts 4:12 points salvation to Jesus. Christians believe He is not only the Lord Peter and John testified about, but the Savior people today can trust.

整篇故事的核心 The heart of the story

彼得和约翰并不是以自己为中心。他们不断把人的注意力指向耶稣：祂曾被钉十字架，神使祂从死里复活，而救恩在祂里面。对他们来说，耶稣不是一个理念，而是他们所见证的复活之主，是救主。 Peter and John did not center the message on themselves. Again and again they pointed people to Jesus: He had been crucified, God raised Him from the dead, and salvation is found in Him. To them, Jesus was not an idea, but the risen Lord they were witnessing about—the Savior.
