使徒行传的重点不是：“彼得和约翰真勇敢。”重点是：他们确信自己所见证的是真实的，并且相信神的灵在他们里面工作。 Acts does not merely say, “Peter and John were brave.” Its emphasis is that they were convinced of what they had witnessed and believed the Spirit of God was at work in them.
