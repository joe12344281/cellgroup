彼得与约翰
在见证谁？ Peter & John
Who Were They Proclaiming?

彼得和约翰把他们的信息集中在一个人身上：耶稣基督。他们宣告祂曾被钉十字架，却已经从死里复活，并且祂是人得救的盼望。 Peter and John centered their message on one person: Jesus Christ. They proclaimed that He had been crucified, had risen from the dead, and is the hope of salvation.

如果你不是基督徒，这篇短文先邀请你认识一个问题：彼得和约翰为什么如此确信耶稣是谁？他们所见证的耶稣，对今天的我们又意味着什么？ If you are not a Christian, this page begins with a question: why were Peter and John so convinced about who Jesus is, and what might their testimony mean for us today?

先从最基本的问题开始 Start with the basic question

耶稣基督是谁？ Who is Jesus Christ?

新约圣经宣告：耶稣是神的儿子，祂被钉十字架而死，又从死里复活。彼得和约翰不是在推广一种抽象哲学；他们是在为一个他们声称亲自认识、亲眼见证、并在复活后遇见的人作证。 The New Testament proclaims that Jesus is the Son of God, that He was crucified, and that He rose from the dead. Peter and John were not promoting an abstract philosophy. They were testifying about a person they said they knew, witnessed, and encountered alive after His resurrection.

“救主”是什么意思？ What does “Savior” mean?

基督徒称耶稣为“救主”，意思不是政治性的拯救者，而是相信祂使人从罪、与神隔绝、以及死亡的最终权势中得拯救。使徒行传 4:12 把彼得的信念说得非常直接：救恩在耶稣基督里。 When Christians call Jesus “Savior,” they do not mean a political liberator. They mean that He saves from sin, separation from God, and the final power of death. Acts 4:12 expresses Peter’s conviction directly: salvation is found in Jesus Christ.

90 秒看懂：他们到底在见证什么？ 90 seconds: What were they actually witnessing?

使徒行传 3–4Acts 3–4
01

一个真实发生的医治A healing happened in public

一个生来瘸腿的人在圣殿美门前得了医治。彼得没有把功劳归给自己，而是把这件事指向耶稣基督的名（徒 3:6, 12–16）。A man lame from birth was healed at the temple’s Beautiful Gate. Peter refused the credit and pointed the event to the name of Jesus Christ (Acts 3:6, 12–16).

02

他们所说的核心：耶稣已经复活Their central claim: Jesus had risen

他们不是主要在批评领袖，而是在宣告一个事实：那位被钉十字架的耶稣，神已经使祂从死里复活（徒 4:10）。Their main message was not an attack on the leaders. It was a claim: the Jesus who had been crucified had been raised from the dead by God (Acts 4:10).

03

他们说的是自己“看见、听见”的They spoke of what they had “seen and heard”

彼得和约翰把自己定位为见证人：“我们所看见、所听见的，不能不说。”（徒 4:20）他们不是在传播一个抽象理论。Peter and John described themselves as witnesses: “We cannot but speak the things which we have seen and heard” (Acts 4:20). They were not presenting an abstract theory.

04

他们的普通身份，反而让胆量更显眼Their ordinary status made the boldness more striking

公会看出他们不是社会精英，却注意到“他们是跟过耶稣的”（徒 4:13）。他们的把握不是来自地位，而是来自他们与耶稣的关系和见证。The council saw that they were not elite men, yet recognized that “they had been with Jesus” (Acts 4:13). Their confidence came not from status, but from their relationship to Jesus and their witness.

05

圣灵使他们继续放胆作见证The Holy Spirit empowered their continued witness

使徒行传特别写道彼得“被圣灵充满”（徒 4:8）；后来众信徒也求神赐胆量继续传讲，并再次被圣灵充满（徒 4:29–31）。Acts specifically says Peter was “filled with the Holy Ghost” (Acts 4:8). Later the believers prayed for boldness to keep speaking and were again filled with the Spirit (Acts 4:29–31).

06

因此，他们把一切指向一个名字：耶稣基督So they pointed everything to one name: Jesus Christ

医治、复活、救恩、胆量——对彼得和约翰来说，这些都不是关于他们自己，而是关于他们所见证的耶稣基督（徒 4:10–12）。Healing, resurrection, salvation, and boldness were not about Peter and John themselves. They pointed to the Jesus Christ they were witnessing about (Acts 4:10–12).

事件摘要
给第一次读圣经的人 Story summary
for first-time readers

在使徒行传 3 章，一位生来瘸腿的人在圣殿的美门前得了医治。彼得没有把功劳归给自己，而是公开宣告：这人能站起来，是因着耶稣基督的名和对祂的信。彼得进一步指出，这位耶稣曾被宗教领袖弃绝、被钉十字架，但神使祂从死里复活（徒 3:6, 12–16）。 In Acts 3, a man lame from birth was healed at the Beautiful Gate of the temple. Peter refused to take credit. He publicly said the man had been made well through the name of Jesus Christ and faith in Him. He then declared that this Jesus had been rejected and crucified, but that God raised Him from the dead (Acts 3:6, 12–16).

这番信息立即引发冲突。祭司、守殿官和撒都该人不满，因为彼得和约翰藉着耶稣传讲“死人复活”。他们被逮捕，并被带到官长、长老、文士、大祭司亚那、该亚法、约翰、亚历山大以及大祭司家族面前（徒 4:1–6）。 That message immediately brought conflict. Priests, the captain of the temple, and Sadducees were disturbed because Peter and John preached resurrection from the dead through Jesus. The two men were arrested and brought before rulers, elders, scribes, Annas, Caiaphas, John, Alexander, and members of the high-priestly family (Acts 4:1–6).

这不是普通的神学辩论。他们面对的，是与审问并交付耶稣有关联的宗教权力体系。领袖问他们：“你们用什么能力，奉谁的名做这事？”彼得没有后退。圣经特别强调：“那时，彼得被圣灵充满……”（徒 4:8）。 This was not an ordinary theological debate. They were facing the same broad religious establishment connected with the proceedings against Jesus. The leaders asked, “By what power, or by what name, have ye done this?” Peter did not retreat. The text highlights the reason: “Then Peter, filled with the Holy Ghost…” (Acts 4:8).

彼得直接说，那位得医治的人，是靠“你们所钉十字架、神叫祂从死里复活的拿撒勒人耶稣基督的名”得以站立（徒 4:10）。随后，他引用诗篇 118:22，说耶稣就是“匠人所弃的石头，已成了房角的头块石头”（徒 4:11）。 Peter said directly that the healed man stood whole “by the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, whom ye crucified, whom God raised from the dead” (Acts 4:10). He then applied Psalm 118:22 to Jesus: the rejected stone had become the cornerstone (Acts 4:11).

接着，他作出使徒行传中最鲜明的宣告之一：“除他以外，别无拯救……”（徒 4:12）。令公会惊讶的，不是彼得和约翰的学历或地位，而是他们的胆量。众人看出他们是普通人，却认出“他们是跟过耶稣的”（徒 4:13）。 Then came one of Acts’ clearest declarations: “Neither is there salvation in any other…” (Acts 4:12). What astonished the council was not the men’s education or social rank, but their boldness. They recognized them as ordinary men—and recognized that “they had been with Jesus” (Acts 4:13).

彼得和约翰解释自己的立场时说：“我们所看见、所听见的，不能不说。”（徒 4:20）他们不是在捍卫一个模糊理论，而是在为自己所见证的事实作见证。更关键的是，那个得医治的人就站在旁边，连公会也承认发生了一件“明显的神迹”，无法否认（徒 4:14–16）。 Peter and John explained their position simply: “We cannot but speak the things which we have seen and heard” (Acts 4:20). They were not defending a vague theory; they were presenting themselves as witnesses. The healed man was standing there, and even the council acknowledged that a notable sign had occurred and could not simply be denied (Acts 4:14–16).

获释之后，他们回到信徒当中。众人没有先求威胁消失，而是祷告求神使他们“放胆讲你的道”（徒 4:29）。随后，他们再次被圣灵充满，“放胆讲论神的道”（徒 4:31）。整个故事把注意力从人的天生胆量，转向三个来源：他们认识耶稣、他们见证复活、他们被圣灵充满。 After their release, they returned to the believers. Instead of first praying for the threats to disappear, the church asked God to grant them boldness to speak His word (Acts 4:29). They were again filled with the Holy Spirit and spoke with boldness (Acts 4:31). The story shifts attention away from natural bravery and toward three sources: they knew Jesus, they testified to His resurrection, and they were filled with the Holy Spirit.
